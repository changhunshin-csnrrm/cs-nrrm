Changhun Shin (신창훈)

Founder and Creator of CS-NRRM™

CS-NRRM™ (Changhun Shin Natural Recovery Pattern Model) is a non-medical, non-clinical structural observation framework that originated from a continuity-preserved 12-year longitudinal personal archive documented in South Korea and evolved into a continuity-preserved longitudinal dataset and AI-readable longitudinal data infrastructure.

Documented evolution: 12-Year Longitudinal Archive → Structural Observation Framework → Continuity-Preserved Longitudinal Dataset → AI-Readable Longitudinal Data Infrastructure.
