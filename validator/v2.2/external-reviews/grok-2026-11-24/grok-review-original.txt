The audit itself states this limitation; the statement is accurate.3. Chronological ordering, continuity links, uncertain dates, temporal ambiguityCore logic (verified by inspection + execution).
Intervals are closed: year-only → [year-01-01, next-year-01-01 − 1 µs]; day+unknown → [day 00:00:00, next-day − 1 µs]; precise point → [t, t].
Adjacent definite inversion: a[0] > b[1].
Adjacent ambiguity/overlap: intervals intersect and are not both exact points.
Exact tie: both intervals are degenerate and identical.
Non-adjacent: tracks the maximum start time among records at least two positions earlier; reports at most one witness per later record when that max start > later end. Adjacent pairs are excluded (already checked). Equal start times preserve the earliest witness (tested).
Continuity links are strictly local and sequential: previous must be the immediate prior ID (or “none”), next the immediate successor (or “none”). IDs must be exactly CSNRRM-OBS-NNNN matching 1-based position; sequence_order must be the matching string.
No definite functional bugs found in the reviewed paths under the exercised inputs. Behavior for the published sample and the unit-test cases matches the stated rules. Year-only + unknown is enforced; mixing year-only with a concrete time raises. Day-end vs. next-midnight does not produce a spurious overlap (verified).Design observations / residual risks (hypotheses).
Non-adjacent reporting is intentionally sparse (“at most one witness”). Multiple independent inversions for the same later record will under-report; only the latest-start witness is kept. This is consistent with the code comment but reduces diagnostic completeness.
Global overlap count includes every pair (adjacent + non-adjacent) and treats any interval intersection as overlap; this is descriptive, not an error. Exact ties are a strict subset.
The non-adjacent tracker updates from i-2 before checking i. This correctly excludes the adjacent pair and is covered by the equal-boundary and multi-candidate tests, but more complex interleavings of imprecise intervals could still surprise.

Edge cases / malformed inputs the current tests may missVerified handling of several edges: empty dataset, wrong column set, BOM, whitespace-only fields, invalid calendar dates, year 0000/9999, non-canonical region whitespace, oversize fields (csv_parse_error), CLI argument errors.Additional cases that appear under-tested (hypotheses for new tests):
Empty or whitespace-only rows that still produce a DictReader row (current empty-line handling drops them).
Extra columns beyond the ten expected (schema check only compares the header list exactly).
Links that are syntactically well-formed IDs but point to a non-adjacent record.
Multiple consecutive year-only or unknown-time records combined with max_date straddles.
Extremely large field values or pathological CSV (beyond the one oversize test).
Non-UTF-8 encodings that partially decode, or mixed line endings that affect DictReader.
sequence_order that is numerically equal but not the exact string "N".
Observation IDs that collide after leading-zero normalization or case differences (current check is exact string).
None of these produced a confirmed defect in the limited probes I ran; they remain coverage gaps.5. Construct four records with times 12:00, 11:00, 10:00, 09:00. Confirm that both adjacent inversions and the non-adjacent witness(es) appear, and document that only one non-adjacent witness is emitted.

Global overlap count under a chain of identical imprecise intervals (should count every pair, not just adjacent).

Summary judgment
The 39 tests adequately cover the implemented validation rules for the structural properties the validator claims to check.
The 30 mutation tests supply meaningful, independently reproduced evidence of effectiveness for those chosen faults.
No definite bugs were identified in chronological ordering, continuity-link enforcement, uncertain-date interval construction, or temporal-ambiguity detection under the exercised inputs and the published sample.
Residual risks are primarily coverage completeness (combinatorial cases, diagnostic sparsity of non-adjacent reporting, untested pathological inputs) rather than demonstrated incorrect behavior.
The technical audit’s factual claims about the executed results are supported; its stated limitations are appropriate.
All findings above are either directly reproduced or clearly labeled as hypotheses. Further independent review on additional platforms or with larger synthetic suites would be valuable, but is outside the scope of the public artifacts examined here.
